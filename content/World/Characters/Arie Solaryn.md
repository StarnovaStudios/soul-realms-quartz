Arie Solaryn, otherwise known as **The Emperor** or **The Shadow of the Star**, is the current holder of the [[The Eye]] and widely considered the most powerful [[Arsborne]] in history – living and dead. He possesses immense durability, strength, and mana of a magnitude incomparable by any other being. He is widely considered the first and last line of defense preventing corruption from seeping into the Ars.
