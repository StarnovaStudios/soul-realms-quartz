### Joining Soul Realms
In order to get started, you'll have to join us first! You can view more in-depth Join Guides in [[How to Join Soul Realms]] but we'll explain some basics here.

- **Java Edition:** On versions 1.21.4 or higher, join us with IP `play.soulrealms.net`
- **Bedrock Edition:** Friend `SoulRealmsMC` in Minecraft and join their game!

Once you join, you'll be in and ready to explore the Soul Realms world! Otherwise known as the [[Ars]]!
