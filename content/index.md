## 🪴What is Soul Realms?
Soul Realms is a vibrant, lore-rich Survival-RPG Minecraft server built by [Starnova Studios](https://starnova.dev/) and lovingly maintained by its passionate player community. Both Java & Bedrock Edition compatible!

## 🧭 Wiki Navigation
The Soul Realms Wiki is divided primarily into two sectors – the Server and the World.
#### The Server
The Server mainly contains information about playing Soul Realms, such as guides, feature information, and more! It contains pages with tag #server 
#### The World
The World focuses on the Soul Realms world and backstory – it includes information about the History of the Ars, characters, entities, locations, and more! Its' separated into its' own "World" folder on the side or under the #world tag!

Both of these regularly intersect! Pages involving the Server focus on the *gameplay* aspect of Soul Realms while the World discusses the *lore and history* of our world!
